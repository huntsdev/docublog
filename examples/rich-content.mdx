This is **illustrative starter content**, showing authoring features rather than a personal achievement. Most posts can stay in plain `.md`. Use `.mdx` when a post needs a reusable component.

## Callouts

<Callout title="A little context">

Callouts accept normal Markdown, including **emphasis**, [links](/blog/), and short lists. Their labels and icons communicate the purpose without relying only on color.

</Callout>

<Callout variant="tip" title="Keep the question close">

Write down the problem before collecting commands and output. Your future self will have a clearer place to start.

</Callout>

<Callout variant="warning" title="Review before publishing">

Replace example identifiers and remove secrets or private infrastructure details from any public notes.

</Callout>

## Responsive embeds

Copy the provider's **embed URL**, rather than a normal watch or sharing link. A descriptive title is required; captions are optional. Unsupported providers and invalid URLs produce a clear build error.

<Embed
  src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/M7lc1UVf-VE"
  title="Google for Developers: YouTube embedded player customization"
  caption="A public demonstration of the YouTube embedded player."
/>

The same component also accepts Vimeo, CodePen, and Spotify embed URLs:

```mdx
<Embed
  src="https://player.vimeo.com/video/76979871"
  title="Vimeo player demonstration"
/>

<Embed
  src="https://codepen.io/chriscoyier/embed/gfdDu"
  title="CodePen embedded example"
/>

<Embed
  src="https://open.spotify.com/embed/track/0Lr4kGOYn9l83EjuK6cZFQ"
  title="Spotify audio player example"
/>
```

These examples reference third-party content. Choose content you want to share and replace each title with a useful description of that specific item.
