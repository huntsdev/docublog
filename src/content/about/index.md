Welcome to **hunts.dev** — a home for technical notes, practical experiments, and projects worth sharing.

This is **illustrative starter content**. Replace this introduction with your own background, interests, and preferred ways to connect. It intentionally makes no claims about employment, credentials, or completed work.

## What you'll find here

The site brings a documentation-style structure to a personal blog: clear navigation, searchable notes, and room for the details that make an idea useful.

- **Blog posts** explore security, systems, automation, and the craft of keeping good technical notes.
- **Projects** collect the purpose, design choices, and progress behind ongoing experiments.
- **Topics** group related posts so you can follow a thread across the archive.

## A note on the writing

The aim is to explain enough context that someone else can understand the problem, try the example in an appropriate environment, and see the limits of the result.

> Useful technical writing leaves the next person with a clearer starting point.

Examples on a public site should use test data and avoid exposing secrets or private infrastructure details. Where a post describes an experiment, record the environment and distinguish observations from assumptions.

## Make yourself at home

Start with the [blog](/blog/), browse the [projects](/projects/), or use search to jump directly to a topic. Social links live in the sidebar; configure them with your own profiles before sharing the site.
